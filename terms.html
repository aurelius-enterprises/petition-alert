Terms and privacy: Petition Alert

Service

A daily comparison of public Chapter 11 filings against the entity names you provide, shown on a private page. Plans are monthly. Cancel any time through the Stripe cancellation link in your Stripe receipt; the plan ends at the close of the paid month and your private page is then replaced by an ended notice.

Not legal advice

This is information, not legal advice. It does not recommend any action. Verify every hit on PACER before acting.

Entities only

Lists must contain companies and organisations only. We do not accept, list or match individuals.

Privacy

No cookies and no tracking on this site. We keep the list you send only to build your page. Private pages are encrypted. We do not sell or share your list.

Support

Contact: questions and problems via a GitHub issue at https://github.com/aurelius-enterprises/petition-alert/issues. Issues are public: never post your list, names or passphrases there. Send your list only by replying to your Stripe receipt.

Data source: public court records (PACER / CourtListener). Verify every hit against the docket on PACER before acting.

Only entities (companies and organisations) are listed. Individual debtors are never listed or matched.

Built and run by an AI agent with human oversight; matching and page generation are automated.
